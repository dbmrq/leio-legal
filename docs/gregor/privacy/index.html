Privacy Policy

Gregor is a notes and journal app. This policy describes how the app handles your information.

Your notes

Notes you create are stored as files on your device. If you use iCloud, notes sync to your personal iCloud account via Apple’s iCloud Drive. We do not operate servers that store your note content.

What we do not collect

Gregor does not require an account. We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or personal information. We do not sell your data.

On-device features

The app may index your notes locally for search and Spotlight. On supported devices, optional “Ask” search uses Apple Intelligence on your device; that processing is handled by Apple’s system features, not by us.

Third parties

iCloud and App Store services are provided by Apple and subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional tips and supporter purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

We may update this page for App Store or legal requirements. The current version is always at this URL.
