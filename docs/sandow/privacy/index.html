Privacy Policy

Sandow is a gym log app. This policy describes how the app handles your information.

Your workouts

Workouts you log are stored as markdown files on your device. By default they live in the app's iCloud Drive container and sync through your personal iCloud account; if iCloud is unavailable, they stay in the app's local Documents folder. You can also point the vault at any folder you choose. We do not operate servers that store your workout content.

If you set up backups, backup copies are written to the destination you configure (for example a folder in iCloud Drive or a WebDAV server you control).

What we do not collect

Sandow does not require an account. We do not collect advertising identifiers or use analytics for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

Crash diagnostics

Sandow uses Sentry to receive crash and performance diagnostics. When the app crashes or hangs, a report may include the device model, operating system version, app version, and a stack trace. It does not include your workout content, files, or account information (there is no account). We use this data only to diagnose crashes and improve stability; Sentry processes it on our behalf and does not use it for advertising.

Apple Intelligence

Converting imported workout notes uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models. Imported content is processed on your device and is not sent to us.

Third parties

iCloud and App Store services are provided by Apple and subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional tips and supporter purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

We may update this page for App Store or legal requirements. The current version is always at this URL.
