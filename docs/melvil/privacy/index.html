Privacy Policy

Melvil is a lists app. This policy describes how the app handles your information.

Your lists

Lists you create are stored as markdown files on your device. If you use iCloud, lists sync to your personal iCloud account via Apple’s iCloud Drive. We do not operate servers that store your list content.

When you share a collection with others, Apple’s iCloud sharing manages participants. Shared list files live in iCloud Drive and are visible to people you invite.

What we do not collect

Melvil does not require an account. We do not collect advertising identifiers or use analytics for advertising, and we do not sell your data.

Crash diagnostics

Melvil uses Sentry to receive crash and performance diagnostics. When the app crashes or hangs, a report may include the device model, operating system version, app version, and a stack trace. It does not include your list content, files, or account information (there is no account). We use this data only to diagnose crashes and improve stability; Sentry processes it on our behalf and does not use it for advertising.

Third parties

iCloud and App Store services are provided by Apple and subject to Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Optional tips and supporter purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

We may update this page for App Store or legal requirements. The current version is always at this URL.
